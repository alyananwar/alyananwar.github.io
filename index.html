About

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of the Pacific. My path started with taking computers apart to see how they worked; that curiosity turned into an IT career securing city infrastructure, then into building AI products that real organizations actually use.

I like problems where I don't know the answer yet. Whether it's a stubborn network issue, a new API, or a hardened VM in a pentest lab, I dig in until I understand not just how something works but why. Lately that means shipping AI-powered tools end to end: product decisions, architecture, code, and deployment.
