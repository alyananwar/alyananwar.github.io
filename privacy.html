Privacy

What this site collects

This site keeps lightweight, anonymous analytics so I can see which projects people find interesting: pages viewed, time on page, scroll depth, referrer, rough location (city/country from the network, never your IP), browser and device type, and a random identifier stored in your browser. No cookies, no fingerprinting, no ad networks, and your IP address is never stored. If your browser sends Do Not Track, nothing is collected at all.

The Connect form

Anything you type into the Connect form (name, email, company, message, and so on) is shared voluntarily and visible only to me. If analytics were active, your form submission is linked to your anonymous visit history so I have context when I follow up. I use this information only to reply and keep in touch — it is never sold or shared.

AI processing

To help me follow up thoughtfully, information you share plus your on-site activity may be summarized by AI services. Only data you provided or generated on this site is used.

Deletion

Want your info gone? Email a_anwar5@u.pacific.edu and I'll delete your contact record and any linked visit history. Raw analytics events are automatically deleted after 180 days regardless.
